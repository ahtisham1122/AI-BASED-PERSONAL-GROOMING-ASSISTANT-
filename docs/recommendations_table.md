## Hairstyle (3 options per face shape, per gender)

### Male
**Oval** (OK)
- Textured crop — Keeps proportions balanced, works with any hairline
- Quiff — Adds height on top without overwhelming your face
- Side part — Clean and classic, flatters even proportions

**Round** (OK)
- High fade — Removes width at the sides, elongates the face
- Pompadour — Vertical volume counters the roundness
- Angular fringe — Sharp lines break up soft, curved features

**Square** (OK)
- Crew cut — Complements a strong jaw without competing with it
- Textured pompadour — Height softens the squareness at the temples
- Side-swept fringe — Softens a strong forehead line

**Heart** (OK)
- Side part — Balances a wider forehead with a narrower chin
- Short textured crop — Keeps volume low, away from the forehead
- Fringe / bangs — Shortens the forehead visually

**Oblong** (OK)
- Fringe / bangs — Shortens the apparent length of the face
- Crew cut with texture — Short sides avoid stretching the face further
- Side part, low volume — Avoids extra height that lengthens the face

**Oblong** (OK)

## Grooming / Beard Tips (2+ per face shape, per gender)

**Square** (OK)

**Heart** (OK)

**Oblong** (OK)
